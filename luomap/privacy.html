Luomap Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026 · Applies to the Luomap app for Android, published on Google Play by Ogaluo.

The short version

  • Luomap has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Everything you create in the app (visits, notes, edits, layers) stays on your device. We never receive it.
  • To draw the map, the app downloads map images from a map provider, Geoapify. Like any web request, that reveals which area you are viewing and your IP address to them.

1. What the app stores on your device

Luomap keeps the following in its private storage on your phone, where other apps cannot read it:

  • places you mark as visited, and the dates;
  • names, descriptions and categories you edit, points you add or delete, and layers you create, rename or delete;
  • KMZ or KML files you import;
  • display preferences, such as which layers are shown;
  • a cache of map images, so areas you have already looked at load faster.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

2. Your location

If you grant the location permission, Luomap uses your position to show a "you are here" dot and to centre the map on you. Your position is used only on the device. The app does not send it anywhere and does not keep a history of where you have been.

The permission is optional. If you decline it, everything else in the app keeps working, without the dot.

Note that when the map is showing your surroundings, the map images it requests (see the next section) are of that area.

3. Map images from Geoapify

The map itself is made of images ("tiles") that the app downloads from Geoapify while you use it. Each request tells Geoapify which part of the map is being viewed and at what zoom level. As with any internet request, it also includes your device's IP address, plus the app's name and version.

Geoapify states that it keeps request information, including the IP address, for no longer than 24 hours, for access control and usage counting. See the Geoapify privacy policy. We do not receive or see this information.

4. Things that take you outside the app

Some actions only happen when you choose them, and hand information to another app or service:

  • Directions opens your navigation app with the coordinates of the place you selected. If you have none installed, it opens Google Maps in your browser instead. That app's or website's own privacy policy applies from there.
  • Links in place descriptions open in your browser.
  • Import and export use Android's standard file picker. Files are read from, or saved to, the location you pick, which may be a cloud storage service you use. Luomap itself does not upload anything.

5. Backups

If backup is turned on for your device, Android may include your Luomap data (the items in section 1, except the map image cache) in your device backup with Google, so that it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is managed by Android and your Google account, not by us. You can turn it off in your device settings.

6. What we don't do

  • We don't ask for your name, email or any other personal details. There is no sign-in.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share personal data. The app never sends us any.

7. Deleting your data

Because your data stays on your device, you are in full control of it. To delete it, uninstall Luomap, or clear its storage in your phone's settings (Settings → Apps → Luomap → Storage → Clear storage). Device backups can be managed from your Google account. We hold nothing about you, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

8. Children

Luomap is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, children included.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Luomap changes how data is handled (for example by adding optional purchases), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
